TRAVEL SMART

A little planning. A lot more freedom.

Use these practical pointers to plan a trip that suits your pace.

THE ESSENTIALS

Before you set off.

A flexible plan makes it easier to enjoy your time away.

01

Choose your season

Weather varies across Japan. Spring and autumn can be popular, while summer brings heat and rain in many regions. Check conditions for each destination.

02

Check entry requirements

Visa rules and passport requirements depend on nationality and can change. Verify the latest guidance with the relevant Japanese embassy or official immigration source.

03

Map out your connections

Major cities are linked by trains, but rural areas may need local buses or advance planning. Compare routes and transport passes for your own itinerary.

04

Plan for everyday needs

Consider mobile connectivity, travel insurance, accessibility needs, payment methods and emergency contacts. Keep essential details available offline.

A helpful reminder

This student-produced guide offers general inspiration, not live travel advisories. Always verify prices, schedules, transport status and entry rules through official sources before booking.
