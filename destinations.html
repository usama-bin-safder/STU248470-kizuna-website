EXPLORE THE COUNTRY

Where will curiosity take you?

Five different settings. Endless ways to feel the spirit of Japan.

DESTINATION 01

Tokyo

A city of neighborhoods

Explore the contrast between bustling shopping districts, contemporary architecture and tranquil parks. Choose one or two neighborhoods per day rather than crossing the whole city repeatedly.

Ideas: neighborhood walks · museums · food markets

Plan a visit →

DESTINATION 02

Kyoto

Tradition in everyday life

Discover gardens, shrines, and historic streets. Visit popular sites early, dress respectfully at places of worship, and seek out lesser-known areas to escape peak crowds.

Ideas: temple walks · gardens · craft workshops

Plan a visit →

DESTINATION 03

Mount Fuji & the Five Lakes

Mountains and lakeside calm

The Fuji Five Lakes region rewards patient travelers with outdoor trails and scenic viewpoints. Mountain visibility changes with weather, so build flexibility into your itinerary.

Ideas: lakeside cycling · viewpoints · local cafés

Plan a visit →

DESTINATION 04

Okinawa

Island horizons

A subtropical escape with distinctive traditions, coral-blue water and time for a slower itinerary. Consider seasonal conditions before planning swimming or boat trips.

Ideas: beaches · cultural sites · coastal strolls

Plan a visit →

DESTINATION 05

Japanese Alps

The quieter side of Japan

Mountain towns, crisp air and striking landscapes offer a different pace. Routes and access can vary significantly by season, especially during winter.

Ideas: village walks · hiking · scenic routes
